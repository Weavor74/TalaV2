Tool: vault_list

<p><strong>vault_list</strong> is a callable tool in Tala's registry. List the user's private vault documents: id, title and tags. Never returns contents -- use vault_read to open one.</p><p>It takes no arguments: <code>vault_list()</code>.</p><p>Titles and tags only. Enough to know a document exists and decide whether to open it, without opening everything to find out. Not gated on the active model: a vault whose titles cannot be seen is a vault nobody can ask about. The bodies are what the grant governs.</p><p>Implemented in <code>sdk.agent.vault_tools</code>. The Python registry remains what actually executes; this node is the catalogue entry that makes the tool retrievable and referenceable from a workflow.</p>
