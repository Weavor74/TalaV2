Emotion Vocabulary Specification

This node is the canonical vocabulary of every emotion Tala's astro-emotional engine can name, together with each emotion's projection onto the four valence keys (joy, caution, curiosity, neutral) that update_manifest and adjust_behavioral_weights read. An emotion the engine can emit but this node does not list produces a state that cannot be projected, so the two are kept in agreement by a test rather than by discipline.

The source of truth for the words themselves is PLANET_EMOTION_MAP in python/astrology/emotion.py: each planet carries a harmonious and a challenging palette, and aspect quality selects between them. This node was regenerated from that map on 2026-08-12, after the original was destroyed by the rollback incident recorded in the recovery commit.
