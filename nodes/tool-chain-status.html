Tool: chain_status

<p><strong>chain_status</strong> is a callable tool in Tala's registry. Check the health of your local blockchain node: whether it is reachable, its block height, whether the whole chain validates, and its newest block. Read-only.</p><p>It takes no arguments: <code>chain_status()</code>.</p><p>Asks the node for its status. Reports ``reachable``, ``height``, ``is_valid`` (the node's own full recheck), ``latest_block_hash`` (first 12 characters), ``pending_transactions`` and ``peer_count``. A node that is down returns ``reachable: False`` with a hint; it never raises.</p><p>Implemented in <code>sdk.agent.chain_tools</code>. The Python registry remains what actually executes; this node is the catalogue entry that makes the tool retrievable and referenceable from a workflow.</p>
