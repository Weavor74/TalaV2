Tool: schedule_goal

<p><strong>schedule_goal</strong> is a callable tool in Tala's registry. Schedule a recurring autonomous goal on a cron expression (5-field, e.g. '0 8 * * *' = daily 8am). The goal runs unattended through the GoalEngine and can use any tool, including messaging channels.</p><p>Call it as <code>schedule_goal(name*, cron_expr*, goal_text*, enabled)</code>, where <code>*</code> marks a required argument:</p><ul><li><code>name</code> (string, required)</li><li><code>cron_expr</code> (string, required)</li><li><code>goal_text</code> (string, required)</li><li><code>enabled</code> (string, optional)</li></ul><p>Persists a heartbeat job and hot-reloads the scheduler. Standard 5-field cron syntax (minute hour day-of-month month day-of-week). Examples: '0 8 * * *' daily 8:00, '*/30 * * * *' every 30 min, '0 9 * * 1' Mondays 9:00.</p><p>Implemented in <code>sdk.agent.tools</code>. The Python registry remains what actually executes; this node is the catalogue entry that makes the tool retrievable and referenceable from a workflow.</p>
